Privacy policy

Last updated [date]

Sharelith helps you split expenses with friends and keep track of who owes whom. This policy explains what information the app stores, why, and the choices you have.

What we store

  • Your account: your name, email address and a password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider.
  • Your profile: optionally a phone number and a profile photo, plus your default currency and time zone.
  • What you share in the app: your friends, groups, expenses, how each expense is split, payments you record, and a history of these changes.
  • People you add: when you add a friend by email who isn't on Sharelith yet, we store their email address and the name you give, and send them one invitation email.

We don't collect your location or contacts, we don't show ads, and the app contains no advertising or analytics trackers.

Who can see it

  • Your friends and the members of your groups see your name, email address and profile photo.
  • People on an expense see that expense, who paid and what each person owes. Members of a group see the group's expenses.
  • Your phone number, time zone and other settings are visible only to you.

Why we use it

Only to run Sharelith: to work out balances, show your activity, keep your devices in sync, and send account emails (confirming your email, resetting your password, telling you when someone adds you as a friend). We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in and file storage, in [region].
  • Mailtrap sends account emails.
  • [Crash reporting service, if added] receives technical details when the app crashes.

On your device

Your sign-in session is kept in the device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android). A copy of your recent data is cached on the device so the app opens quickly; it's removed when you log out.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can change your profile at any time, and delete your account in the app under Account → Delete account. You need to settle up first, so nobody is left with a balance they can't clear.

When you delete your account, your sign-in, email address, phone number and photo are removed. Expenses you shared stay in other people's records, shown under "Deleted user", so their balances remain correct.
