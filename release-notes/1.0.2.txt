Sharelith 1.0.2

New
- Ads in the free version: a small banner at the bottom of Groups, Friends and Activity, the occasional sponsored item in Activity (marked "Ad"), and rarely a full-screen ad after adding an expense (never in your first few, at most once every 5 minutes). Ads never appear while you're filling in a form.
- Ad choices: in the EU, UK and Switzerland you're asked how ads may use your data, with a clear "Do not consent" option. You can change it any time under Account > About > Ad choices.

Fixed
- The app could close itself right after opening. That's fixed.

Note
- Ads may be blank for a while as they start up. If something looks wrong, please send a short description, since screenshots of the app are blocked to protect your data.
