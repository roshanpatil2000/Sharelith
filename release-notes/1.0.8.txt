Sharelith 1.0.8

New
- A new bottom bar: it floats above the bottom of the screen, and the tab you pick rises in a circle.

Changed
- Screenshots and screen recording now work on Android. Please attach a screenshot when you report a problem.
- Because of that, your balances and contacts can now show in the recent-apps preview, in recordings and when you share your screen. Take care before sharing.

Fixed
- If you were given Pro and had an older subscription that ended, the Pro screen no longer shows a renewal date in the past.

Note
- Please mention your app version (Account screen) when you report a problem.
