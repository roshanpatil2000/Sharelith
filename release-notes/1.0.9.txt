Sharelith 1.0.9

New
- After you log in or sign up: three short welcome pages, then a choice to plan a trip, share a home, or look around first.
- Add expense is now a full screen: pick who it's with, then "Paid by you and split equally" reads as a sentence. Date and notes sit at the bottom.
- Create group: choose a type (trip, home, couple or other) with one tap.
- An Appearance screen in Account for light, dark or your phone's setting.

Changed
- Fresh layouts for group and expense details, with Edit and Delete at the top of an expense.
- Larger titles on the main tabs, and illustrations when a list is empty.
- Each way to split now explains itself (equal, exact, percentage, shares, plus or minus, items).
- Before asking for your contacts, Sharelith explains why.
- Smoother movement between screens.
- Ads appear a little more often in the free version.

Fixed
- Choosing who paid, how to split, a category, currency or date no longer leaves the keyboard covering the options.

Note
- Please mention your app version (Account screen) when you report a problem.
