Sharelith 1.0.5

Important
- Test accounts and data were reset. Please sign up again (it takes a minute), and re-add your friends and groups.

New
- Sharelith Pro: no ads, receipt photos, export to CSV or PDF, and spending insights. Account > Sharelith Pro shows what's included. Buying isn't open yet in this test build.
- Categories are picked for you from what you type: "Lunch" is Food, "Uber" is Transport, "Netflix" is Entertainment. You can still change it.
- Sign-in alerts: we email you after every sign-in with the device and approximate location.

Changed
- Sharelith stays signed in on one device at a time. Signing in on another phone signs the old one out, with a message saying why.
- A new logo on the welcome and loading screens.
- Your cached data on the phone is now encrypted.
- Signing up with an email that already has an account now says so.

Note
- Please mention your app version (Account screen) when you report a problem.
