Sharelith 1.0.4

New
- A fresh app icon and launch screen: a coin split in two, for shared bills.

Changed
- The ad banner is gone. Instead, an occasional full-screen ad may appear at a natural break, such as after adding an expense or moving between screens. It never shows during your first few moves and at most once every few minutes.
- Groups and Friends now show the same small in-list ads as Activity, spaced well apart.
